How to Use

1. Upload Your Data

Click "Choose Files" and select your Excel or CSV files. We support multiple files and sheets.

2. Ask Your Question

Just type what you want to see in plain English:

  • "Show me sales by region"
  • "Compare revenue across product types"
  • "Average price per category and month"
  • "How many customers in each city?"

3. Get Instant Insights

We'll automatically create charts and tables showing exactly what you asked for. Switch between chart types or export to Excel.

🔒 All data processing happens in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
